Selected work

LogicForge · Jul–Nov 2026 · Commercial product

Address Book: company directory for Android and Windows

A subscription directory of companies and the people in them. Free members see company names; paid members get full contact details, saved items, and reminders. One Compose Multiplatform codebase runs on an Android phone and on a Windows desktop, backed by Supabase.

What I built

  • Card scanning on the device. ML Kit for Latin script, PaddleOCR on ONNX Runtime for Arabic-script and Chinese cards. It pulls out name, company, phone, email and address, and nothing is uploaded.
  • Voice search in 6 languages. Android speech recognition with a check for which languages the phone actually supports, and a clean fallback when it has no speech engine.
  • Offline copy. The directory is cached in an encrypted SQLDelight/SQLCipher database, so it still opens with no connection. The user can switch it off, and doing so erases it.
  • Sign-in. Email OTP and Google OAuth with PKCE. Sessions are stored encrypted, and logs are kept free of personal data.

How I worked on it

  • Clean Architecture with MVVM, Koin, Coroutines and StateFlow. The shared module holds the domain and data layers.
  • 778 JVM unit tests, then a structured QA pass on a real Android phone and on Windows: 37 defects logged, 31 fixed.
  • Search is typo-tolerant and bounded on purpose, so cost stays constant however long the query.
